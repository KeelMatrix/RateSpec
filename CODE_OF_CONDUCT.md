# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone. We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy and kindness, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling or personal attacks, harassment, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and Enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project. Community leaders may take fair corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior.

Concerns about conduct in public project spaces may be raised through the GitHub issue tracker for maintainer review. GitHub's reporting tools may also be used. Do not use the security contact for conduct matters.
